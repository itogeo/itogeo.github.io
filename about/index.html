About Ito Geospatial

A small mapping studio. We build remote sensing pipelines, interactive maps, and StoryMaps for watershed councils, counties, utilities, and research teams.

What we do

Most projects pair a custom data pipeline with a public-facing map — something a board member can open on their phone and a staff engineer can rerun next quarter without calling us.

We're picky about scope. A project is a fit if the answer depends on spatial data, the audience reaches beyond GIS specialists, and you want the pipeline to outlive the contract.

How we work

The core team is small. The person scoping your project is the person writing the pipeline. When a project needs specialists, we bring in long-term collaborators — MSU's Geospatial Core Facility for peer-review rigor, Zenith Economics for policy and funding translation, and independent surveyors and ML engineers when they fit.

Every deliverable ships with documentation, versioned data, and a clean handoff.
